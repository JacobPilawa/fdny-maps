# Fire Think registration follow-up

This focused read-only pass checked the FireRescue callbacks around the unresolved scheduler edge. Decompiled exports and raw instruction context are in this directory; related constructor/vtable exports remain in `../../item-pose/live-extinguisher/message-components/slot-zero-callers/fireresque-helper-exports/`.

- FireRescue constructor `0044A290` installs primary table `004E1780` and secondary table `004E1774` at object `+0x24`. Initialization `0044A330` marks category bit 1 through `0049D330`; that helper only sets the bit at `+0x20`. `0049D160` validates the entity UserData size, and `0049D510` registers the object’s message identity. None of these calls identifies the recurring `00449FE0` invocation.
- The primary `+0x48` callback `004483A0` handles proximity influence and can enqueue `0x7531`. It calls `00448320` to update exposure/progression and the low-threshold factory path; it does not call secondary slot 0 in the preserved instructions.
- `004483A0` only proceeds while the FireRescue state is not 5 and its counter at object `+0xB8` is in `[0,4)`. It obtains the player origin, computes vertical separation with `004B9400` (float-to-integer conversion, not an RNG draw), updates progression, and when the influence threshold passes stores fire-origin/influence in the next `+0xBC` record and queues message `0x7531` to the player's registered route. It then increments `+0xB8`. Secondary Think `00449FE0` runs on object `+0x24` and clears this same count at subobject `+0x94` near return. Thus each FireRescue has a source-backed limit of four queued exposure records between Think resets; the actual number and scheduling order in Training still require a live trace.
- Player handler `00453A70` routes `0x7531` to `00451640`. The `004B9400` conversion in the fire callback must not be counted as a shared RNG draw. In `00451640`, the unsigned compare at `00451748` skips the pain branch while global clock `[0x00502AFC]` is less than or equal to player `+0x390`. Once it is greater, the callback resolves `RC_Pain` through `004897C0` before calling `00498B30(1000,2500)`. A matching chatter set may consume one or two shared RNG draws through `00488D40`; the deadline helper then consumes one additional shared draw, adds its result to the current `[0x00502AFC]`, converts the sum through `004B9400`, and stores the low dword back to `+0x390`. The comparison plus addition identify `+0x390` as a future time deadline; they are clearer in the raw instructions than in Ghidra's decompilation, which omits the x87 current-time addition. See `player-exposure-timer-followup/timer-semantics.md` and `player-exposure-accumulation-audit.md`.
- `00449FE0` remains secondary vtable slot 0. Its state-4 branch calls `00449340` for spread/child-fire work. Static constructor and owner-manager traces still do not establish who schedules this Think callback or its order relative to player/tool updates and message drain.
