# Evidence in the public mirror

This folder includes authored research notes and numeric output fixtures. The working copy also contains raw decompiler exports, instruction listings, Ghidra databases, original retail files and live-capture manifests; those machine-derived or machine-specific files remain local and are excluded by `../.gitignore`.

Status claims in the checklist distinguish component comparisons from live integration. Numeric fixtures compare specific functions or controlled inputs and do not establish complete Training or shared-RNG parity.
