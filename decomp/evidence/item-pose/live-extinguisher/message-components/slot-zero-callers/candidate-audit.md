# Fire Think slot-0 caller audit

Generated 2026-10-11 from the preserved read-only `FDNYFirefighter.exe` Ghidra project. This audit covers the 91 memory-indirect `CALL [register]` sites plus one memory-indirect tail JMP at `0040EFDC`; it does not count bare `CALL register` forms. Ghidra attributes 62 CALL sites to 48 functions; 29 CALL sites are unclaimed. `slot-zero-callers/index.tsv` is an earlier 12-function focused shortlist, not the complete attributed set. The expanded bare-register sweep and its findings are documented in `register-transfer-audit.md`.

## Fire-related and manager paths

- `00447A90` and `00449340` are fire-related factory paths. At `00447E18` and `0044952B`, respectively, each looks up the `RCS_FireRescue` factory through `00429C00`, calls the returned factory object's slot 0, then initializes the returned object. These are construction calls; they do not establish recurring dispatch to the FireRescue visual subobject at `+0x24` / vtable `004E1774`.
- The four slot-0 calls in `00429520` dispatch fixed owner manager fields at `+0x08`, `+0x50`, `+0x98`, and `+0xE0`. Their category and removal behavior is documented in `../owner-vtable-indirect-call-inventory.md`; they are not the Fire Think slot-0 callback.
- `00425990` and `00425D50` are `PlayerManager.cpp` rich-binary-reader paths. Their slot-0 call is among named-node parsing, not a recurring actor update.

## Generic and unclaimed paths

- `00435CB0` walks array entries gated by byte `+0x1c`, but its only recorded caller is `00435D80`, which draws two bitmaps before calling it; this is a render-side callback list. `00470740` is reached from Player method `0044FE20` for the embedded state at `+0x140`; it does not identify FireRescue Think. `0049C5B0` is a linked-list callback walker with no code/data reference to its entry. These receiver paths remain distinct from a proven FireRescue visual-subobject update.
- Focused exports for five unclaimed calls near array/manager code are in `dispatch-traces/`: `00448173`, `00464D4C`, `0049BDEE`, `0049BEF6`, and `0049C870`. They show array-entry cleanup, a one-call wrapper, a removal/category traversal, and a generic queued-node dispatcher; none connects to the Training FireRescue list in the preserved evidence.
- The owner's `+0x3C4` service is another generic slot-0 callback queue: `00429520` drains it through `0049C820`. A fresh owner-field/global-user audit found no FireRescue visual-subobject insertion into this queue. Its callback type is a candidate shape only, not an identified Fire Think path. See `staged-callback-queue-audit.md` and its preserved scans.
- The remaining 24 unclaimed CALL sites have local decoded-instruction windows in `unclaimed-slot-zero-context.txt`. Twenty-two sites cluster in the `00412FA8`–`00415C8E` region; their windows align with neighboring `TReader_Stream` / `Writer_Stream` code and file-reader vtable calls, not a Fire Think list. `0040CF41` is an array-element callback with a refcount-style argument, and `00412234` is an unclaimed stream/interface call. Their function boundaries and owners remain unresolved, so this does not complete the caller audit.
- The sole zero-offset tail JMP, `0040EFDC`, is an unclaimed generic wrapper that calls the supplied object's slot 0. This output does not identify its callers or receiver class.

No inspected site proves a recurring call to Fire Think `00449FE0`, which is slot 0 of secondary vtable `004E1774` at object `+0x24`. Constructor follow-up shows `00432A40` is a release callback and staged-entity service `0049C670` calls primary initialization slots (`00448D60` and base no-op `0043F660` for FireRescue), not Think. The owner's `+0x3C4` slot-0 queue is serviced each frame, but no registration of the FireRescue visual subobject was found. Fire Think's live caller and position relative to player/tool updates and the owner queue drain remain unresolved. These static results narrow candidate paths but do not prove the absence of another dynamic dispatch path; details and raw exports are in `staged-callback-queue-audit.md` and `fireresque-helper-exports/`.

Raw inventories and exports are preserved in this directory, including `callback-owner-references.tsv` and `callback-owner-exports/`. The initial broad matcher output `slot-zero-indirect-transfers-broad.tsv` matched 1,745 arbitrary bracketed CALL/JMP operands and is excluded from the slot-0 inventory. The 91-call count above is the bracketed-memory subset; the later register-form sweep is the broader transfer inventory.
